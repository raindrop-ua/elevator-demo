Small building.
Plenty of states.

Each elevator has its own route and state machine. The dispatcher decides which one handles the next call.

  1. Call an elevator The arrows on each floor set your direction. The button color shows which elevator is assigned. On the first floor, you can go up. On the fifth floor, you can go down.
  2. Pick your destination Click an elevator or its A, B, or C tab, then select a floor in the control panel. You can add multiple stops, even while the elevator is moving.
  3. Watch the transitions Moving → opening → boarding → closing. Once the doors close, the elevator continues its route or returns to idle. A call on its current floor opens the doors without a trip.
  4. Experiment Pause freezes both the simulation and its animation. Speed up time with 2x or 4x. Running a scenario resets the building and starts a repeatable sequence of trips.

The dispatcher considers distance, direction, and queued stops. Wait time is measured in simulation time until the doors fully open. This is a control model: passengers, capacity, and emergency modes are not simulated.
